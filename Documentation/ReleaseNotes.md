April, again 1.0.1 fixes the reported navigation, interaction, display and artwork problems.

- Five individual HD wardrobe sprites replace small atlas tiles. Texture imports preserve full resolution without block compression.
- Corrected Katya and Mari's first-day poses and Mari's misplaced interaction area.
- Infirmary, library and classrooms are reachable from the scene. Off-camera or obscured exits have clear wayfinding links. The lobby classroom door is correctly labelled.
- Updated to Unity 6000.3.21f1 to fix a Windows shutdown crash after screen-reader/UI Automation inspection (UUM-146676).
- Native-resolution borderless fullscreen is the default. Display settings also offer windowed mode, size presets and VSync.
- Choose the protagonist's first and last names. Names persist in saves; long names paginate correctly. Existing saves remain compatible.
- Click the scene outside the textbox to reveal or advance dialogue. HUD, choices and menus retain separate input.
- Reading settings include instant text, skip unread, resume skip after choices and keep auto after clicking. Skip always waits for the player to choose an answer.
- Removed the requested creator/tool and reused-menu-cue credit lines. Required third-party notices remain included.

**Windows 10/11, 64-bit.** Download **AprilAgain-Setup.exe** for a per-user installation, or **AprilAgain-Windows.zip** for the portable version. Extract the entire ZIP before running AprilAgain.exe.

Click or Space advances dialogue. Escape opens the menu. H hides the interface. F5/F9 quick-save/quick-load. Saves are kept outside the installation folder and preserved by updates and uninstallation.

The release is unsigned and has no voice acting. Third-party font and audio notices are included. SHA-256 checksums accompany both downloads.

Verification includes actual UI raycasts over the visible characters and exits, player naming and save compatibility, reading options, all 15 outcome playthroughs, 225 encounter commits, CG/credits transitions and rendered layout checks at 1920×1080 and 1280×720.
