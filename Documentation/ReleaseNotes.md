April, again is a point-and-click school romance visual novel set at Seiran High School in Japan.

- Five classmates, 75 encounters, 225 choices and 15 illustrated endings.
- Explore the campus, follow appointments, make choices and return home to advance the day.
- Save/load, quick saves, read-only skip, auto, history, settings and a persistent CG gallery.
- Rose-and-ivory interface, Nunito ExtraBold, custom game icon and no Unity splash screen.
- Consistent panel margins, inset headings and close controls, a separate map header,
  centered item previews and measured help-popup layouts.

**Windows 10/11, 64-bit.** Download **AprilAgain-Setup.exe** for a per-user installation, or **AprilAgain-Windows.zip** for the portable version. Extract the entire ZIP before running AprilAgain.exe.

Click or Space advances dialogue. Escape opens the menu. H hides the interface. F5/F9 quick-save/quick-load. Saves are kept outside the installation folder and preserved by uninstallation.

The release is unsigned and has no voice acting. Third-party font and audio notices are included. SHA-256 checksums accompany both downloads.

Verification includes 2,102 rule assertions, controller smoke tests, all 15 outcome playthroughs, all 225 choices, ending/CG/credits transitions and rendered layout checks at 1920×1080 and 1280×720. See the repository's Documentation/ReleaseReview for the final report.
