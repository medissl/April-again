# Akira Seojin — complete route

Quick, curious, friendly; connects ordinary problems to economics or games. Talks past her own fatigue. Earnest technical competence, shaky privacy instincts, and difficulty distinguishing romance from friendship. Never a walking list of references.

Production labels in brackets identify score categories; hide them in the game. Read the setup, then the current relationship-temperature beat, one chosen response, and the shared closing. Do not display all alternatives to the player.

Relationship temperature is calculated BEFORE this scene: warm when raw good minus raw bad is at least 3; cool when it is at most -3; mixed otherwise. These lines change delivery, never unlock or skip scenes.

## akira.01 — The Girl With Three Plans

**Location:** Class A · **Time:** 3-hour activity block · **Initial expression:** neutral

**Props:** sticky_notes, project_notebook · **Outfit:** school_uniform

### Scene

*[A teacher pairs the MC with Akira to collect ideas for a first-year information project.]*

**Akira:** You're the map guy! From orientation. I'm Akira Seojin. Either name is fine, except during roll call, apparently.

**MC:** I was hoping the map thing wouldn't become my entire reputation.

**Akira:** Too late. Memorable brand, low customer-acquisition cost. Sorry—sorry, I'm doing the economics thing. Hi.

*[Her glasses slip down. She pushes them up with a finger still holding a sticky note.]*

**Akira:** We need a project people will actually use. I have a room finder, a lunch queue tracker and a system for borrowing stationery.

**MC:** Is that one project or three?

**Akira:** That's what I need a second person for. My ideas don't tell each other no.

*[Her sentences arrive in quick linked bursts. She starts a fourth thought, sees his expression and physically closes her mouth before it escapes.]*

### Decision

#### 1. The room finder sounds useful. [NEUTRAL]

**Akira:** Because of your tragic map history?

**MC:** I prefer 'relevant experience.'

**Akira:** Excellent. We already have a test user.

#### 2. Which problem have people actually asked you about? Let's start there. [GOOD]

**Akira:** Lunch queues. Constantly. Usually while we're standing in one.

**MC:** Then we can ask what information would help.

**Akira:** A small test before we build anything. Yes! Map guy joins the team. We should probably use your actual name before I print badges.

#### 3. Pick whatever will make our project look the most impressive. [BAD]

**Akira:** A live queue tracker would look pretty good...

**MC:** There you go.

**Akira:** Though we should probably find out whether the canteen would even let us use it.

### Continue

*[We spend the activity block making a short survey. Akira fits her first idea on one page, then puts the other two on a separate sheet.]*

*[The teacher approves the anonymous canteen-queue pilot as the first test. The room finder and lending system remain ideas to revisit later, not alternate projects the player has secretly committed to.]*

**MC:** Saving those for later?

**Akira:** For later me. She has unlimited time and better cable management.
