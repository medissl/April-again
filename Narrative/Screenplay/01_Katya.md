# Katya — complete route

Formal and precise; dry humor. Sharpness protects her pride, but cruelty is not her whole personality. Russian slips are rare and understandable in context. She wants earned competence, not rescue or admiration of money.

Production labels in brackets identify score categories; hide them in the game. Read the setup, then the current relationship-temperature beat, one chosen response, and the shared closing. Do not display all alternatives to the player.

Relationship temperature is calculated BEFORE this scene: warm when raw good minus raw bad is at least 3; cool when it is at most -3; mixed otherwise. These lines change delivery, never unlock or skip scenes.

## katya.01 — The Missing Stapler

**Location:** Class B · **Time:** 3-hour activity block · **Initial expression:** neutral

**Props:** forms, folder, stapler · **Outfit:** school_uniform

### Scene

*[Class B is preparing an information table for new students. A teacher assigns the MC and Katya to finish it during the activity block.]*

**Katya:** Those are the spare forms. The completed ones go on the right.

**MC:** Good thing you stopped me. I was about to invent a new filing system.

**Katya:** Most disasters begin with that sort of confidence.

**MC:** I'm Takahashi. We passed each other during orientation.

**Katya:** Takahashi-san. Yes, I remember. You were examining the map upside down.

**MC:** I hoped nobody noticed.

**Katya:** Ekaterina Romanova. Katya will do. Now—can you operate a stapler, or should I lower my expectations again?

**Narration:** She holds up an empty hand. The stapler is missing. For a moment, neither of us speaks.

### Decision

#### 1. I'll check the supply list. You can show me how you want these sorted. [GOOD]

**Katya:** Sensible. Ask before changing the order.

**MC:** Understood. I'll try to keep my dangerous confidence under control.

**Katya:** See that you do.

#### 2. Just give me a stack. I'll copy what you're doing. [NEUTRAL]

**Katya:** That will work, provided you watch carefully.

**MC:** Copying is one of my stronger school skills.

**Katya:** An unfortunate introduction, but a useful one.

#### 3. You seem to have it handled. I'll stay out of your way. [BAD]

**Katya:** Standing aside is not the same as helping.

**MC:** I thought you'd prefer working alone.

**Katya:** I prefer people to complete the work they were assigned.

### Continue

*[We work around the missing stapler with paper clips, then find it inside the empty forms box.]*

**MC:** At least we know it wasn't stolen.

**Katya:** Yes. A great relief to the international stationery market.

**Narration:** Her face stays serious. I'm almost certain that was a joke.

## katya.04 — Not an Audience

**Location:** Sports field / hardcourt · **Time:** 3-hour activity block · **Initial expression:** determined

**Props:** floor_tape, fencing_bag · **Outfit:** school_tracksuit · **Open-campus day only**

**Katya:** An argument that could support almost any mistake.

*[An older club member corrects her shoulder position. She repeats the movement without answering.]*

**MC:** Does that hurt your arm?

**Katya:** Only my dignity. Apparently my shoulder has been betraying me for years.

**Narration:** The instructor leaves us to reset the markers. She looks at the tape, not at me.

### Decision

#### 1. Show me the step once more. I'll put the markers where you actually need them. [GOOD]

**Katya:** Watch the front foot. The rear one follows; it does not chase.

**MC:** Like that?

**Katya:** Approximately. At least nobody is holding a weapon.

#### 2. You looked perfect to me. The instructor was being picky. [BAD]

**Katya:** He was correct. That is why I listen to him.

**MC:** I meant it as a compliment.

**Katya:** Then compliment something you observed.

#### 3. I'll leave you to practice and finish the tape. [NEUTRAL]

**Katya:** Very well. Keep the spacing consistent.

**MC:** No dramatic curves this time.

**Katya:** I would appreciate the restraint.

### Continue

*[Practice fills the afternoon. By the time we collect the markers, the line is straight and her movement is quieter.]*

**Katya:** Better.

**MC:** Your footwork or my tape?

**Katya:** Both. Do not force me to say it twice.
