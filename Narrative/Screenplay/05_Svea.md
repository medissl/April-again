# Kurokawa Svea — complete route

Blunt, relaxed, short replies and dry humor. Gets animated about basketball; otherwise doesn't waste words. Boku self-reference in Japanese; light roughness without constant insults. Actions carry affection. Comfortable with strength and independence, awkward about choosing closeness.

Production labels in brackets identify score categories; hide them in the game. Read the setup, then the current relationship-temperature beat, one chosen response, and the shared closing. Do not display all alternatives to the player.

Relationship temperature is calculated BEFORE this scene: warm when raw good minus raw bad is at least 3; cool when it is at most -3; mixed otherwise. These lines change delivery, never unlock or skip scenes.

## svea.01 — Pass It Here

**Location:** Sports field / hardcourt · **Time:** 3-hour activity block · **Initial expression:** neutral

**Props:** basketball, attendance_sheet, equipment_cart · **Outfit:** school_sports_jacket_and_trousers

### Scene

*[During a supervised sports activity block, a basketball rolls toward the MC. Svea waits by the hardcourt line with one hand raised.]*

**Svea:** Oi. Here. Bounce it.

**MC:** You're very confident in my throwing.

**Svea:** Not anymore. Keep it low. I'd like my nose where it is.

*[He does. She catches the ball and turns toward the hoop, then notices his attendance sheet.]*

**Svea:** You're helping with the practice count?

**MC:** Apparently. Takahashi.

**Svea:** Kurokawa Svea. Svea's fine. Write down who actually shows up, not who says they'll come.

**MC:** Does that happen a lot?

**Svea:** Enough to be a sentence.

### Decision

#### 1. Got it. Tell me if there's a better place to stand. [GOOD]

**Svea:** Outside the line. Unless you want the ball to introduce itself again.

**MC:** Once was enough.

**Svea:** Fast learner.

#### 2. I'll watch from here and check the names. [NEUTRAL]

**Svea:** Fine. Teacher's by the equipment cart if something's missing.

**MC:** Including my ability to recognize anyone?

**Svea:** Point. I'll tell you.

#### 3. You're pretty good for a girl. [BAD]

**Svea:** Pretty good for someone you've watched for eight seconds.

**MC:** I meant—

**Svea:** Yeah. Leave the last part off next time.

### Continue

*[The block passes in drills, names and the occasional ball sent too far. Svea stays to collect the equipment after everyone else starts leaving.]*

**MC:** Do you do everything yourself?

**Svea:** No. You're holding the sheet.

**Narration:** She says it without looking up. I can't decide whether I've been thanked or assigned a permanent position.

*[A ball gets stuck behind the equipment cart. Before he can reach around it blindly, she points out the wheel that will roll over his shoe.]*

**MC:** You could've let me discover that.

**Svea:** Could've. Then I'd have to listen to the noise.

**Narration:** She pushes the cart while I retrieve the ball. It is a very small job for two people. Neither of us makes it larger.

## svea.05 — A Better Question

**Location:** Sports field / hardcourt · **Time:** 3-hour activity block · **Initial expression:** neutral

**Props:** practice_statistics, basketball · **Outfit:** school_sports_jacket_and_trousers

### Scene

*[The MC helps record a practice drill. Svea watches the numbers with more interest than the score.]*

**MC:** You made fewer shots than last time.

**Svea:** Different distance. Faster pass. Can't compare the total without that.

**MC:** Then what should I compare?

*[She takes the sheet and starts explaining foot position, shot selection and why a bad pass makes an ordinary catch look clumsy.]*

**Narration:** Three minutes pass before she realizes she has been talking without interruption.

**Svea:** ...You still listening?

### Relationship beat — after setup, before decision

**If warm:**

**Svea:** I thought of something to show you. Since you actually ask about this stuff.

**If mixed:**

**Svea:** If you're helping, might as well know what we're counting.

**If cool:**

**Svea:** Just tell me if you don't want the explanation. I don't do guessing games.

### Decision

#### 1. Most of it. I might need the short version for the sheet. [NEUTRAL]

**Svea:** Distance, attempts, clean catches. Three columns.

**MC:** That I can do.

**Svea:** Start there. Ask about the rest later.

#### 2. You're more talkative when you're trying to show off. [BAD]

**Svea:** You asked a question.

**MC:** I didn't expect the lecture.

**Svea:** Fine. Write the number. I'll keep the rest.

#### 3. Yeah. Why does the clean catch matter more than the total? [GOOD]

**Svea:** Because it tells me whether the next move started right. The shot's later.

**MC:** So we're checking the part you can fix first.

**Svea:** Exactly. See? Not that complicated.

### Continue

*[The next set is easier to record and harder to shoot. Svea notices one improvement before checking the final total.]*

**Svea:** Better.

**MC:** You or my columns?

**Svea:** Both. Don't get greedy.

*[With the drill finished, she offers him one attempt from the same marker. The ball falls short enough that neither of them can pretend otherwise.]*

**MC:** I think the hoop moved.

**Svea:** Whole building must be in on it. Try bending your knees.

**Narration:** The second try touches the rim. She nods once, a more useful reaction than pretending it went in.

## svea.06 — The Line Was Out

**Location:** Sports field / hardcourt · **Time:** 3-hour activity block · **Initial expression:** annoyed

**Props:** court_cones, basketball · **Outfit:** school_sports_jacket_and_trousers

### Scene

*[During a supervised practice game, a disputed ball gives Svea's side an extra possession. The MC saw the ball cross the line before she caught it.]*

**Svea:** It was out.

**Classmate:** Nobody called it. Keep playing.

**Svea:** I'm calling it now.

*[The instructor resets possession. Svea's side loses the short game by one point.]*

**MC:** That's an annoying margin.

**Svea:** Yeah. Still out.

**Narration:** She starts collecting the cones. Her movements are sharper than usual.

### Decision

#### 1. You could've kept quiet. Winning would've made everyone happier. [BAD]

**Svea:** Not everyone. Other team's in the game too.

**MC:** Your team would've liked it.

**Svea:** They can like a win we actually earned.

#### 2. You made the right call. You can still be annoyed about losing. [GOOD]

**Svea:** Good. Because I'm annoyed.

**MC:** I noticed.

**Svea:** Subtle, wasn't I.

#### 3. Want to look at the last play once you've cooled down? [NEUTRAL]

**Svea:** Later. Need to be annoyed at the cones first.

**MC:** I'll take the innocent ones.

**Svea:** All innocent. That's what makes it difficult.

### Continue

*[By the end of the block, she can point out the missed defensive switch without blaming the line call.]*

**Svea:** Next time I want a bigger margin.

**MC:** So one honest decision won't decide it?

**Svea:** So we play better. Same thing, if we're lucky.
